## Gradient-Based Neck Contours

- `_find_ellipse()` now:
  1. Applies a `5 x 5` Gaussian blur.
  2. Calculates Sobel X and Y gradients.
  3. Combines them into gradient magnitude.
  4. Applies a configurable gradient-magnitude threshold.
  5. Uses a `3 x 3` closing operation to connect small edge gaps.
  6. Extracts contours from the resulting edge mask.
- Renamed the two configuration fields to reflect their actual meaning:

```ini
neck_gradient_threshold_cam1 = [60]
neck_gradient_threshold_cam2 = [60]
```

- Added a regression image whose background brightness already exceeds the old absolute threshold, confirming that Neck detection now depends on local contrast instead of absolute intensity.

## Crescent Outer-Arc And Ellipse Fit

- Continued using `_extract_outer_convex_arc()` to remove the inner concave crescent boundary before fitting.
- Replaced rotated `cv2.fitEllipse()` fitting in the Neck path with `_fit_axis_aligned_ellipse()`.
- The fit excludes the `xy` cross term, so:
  - The horizontal axis always follows image X.
  - The vertical axis always follows image Y.
  - The returned angle is always `0.0`.
- Kept `_ellipse_lower_point()` rotation-aware because other ellipse-based paths can still use rotated geometry.

## Vertex Sensitivity Investigation

- Compared two rapid successive stereo observations:

```text
Frame 1 Cam1: (1084.3665,  701.1736)
Frame 1 Cam2: (1069.7957, 1529.5425)
Frame 2 Cam1: (1085.8788,  693.0339)
Frame 2 Cam2: (1073.6512, 1523.0649)
```

- Recalculated them with `ccal002.json` and the offline crop origin `(1500, 0)`:

```text
Frame 1 vertex Z: 14.1034 mm
Frame 2 vertex Z: 12.9815 mm
Difference:         -1.1219 mm
```

- Isolated the coordinate contributions:

```text
X-coordinate changes only: approximately -0.0135 mm in Z
Y-coordinate changes only: approximately -1.1084 mm in Z
```

- Confirmed that the stereo solver converged normally; the reconstructed height jump was driven almost entirely by the `6-8 px` image-Y movement.
- Identified two causes in the image path:
  - A partial crescent arc weakly constrains the full ellipse center and vertical radius.
  - A thresholded Sobel edge has finite thickness and can move between neighboring edge locations.
