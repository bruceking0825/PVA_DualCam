## Diameter Calculation Review

- Traced the full diameter data flow from `PageHome` through `MeasurementWorker` to `MeasurementEngine`.
- Confirmed that `PageHome` performs image pairing, stage selection, result display, and PLC queuing; the geometric calculation is implemented in `online_core/engine.py`.
- Documented the three stage-specific calculations:
  - Neck: fit the neck ellipse in both views, triangulate the ellipse center and lower point, and calculate twice their XY-plane world distance.
  - Body/Crown: repeat the stereo center/lower-point calculation, optionally subtract the body meniscus correction, and update the pixel scale.
  - Endcone: detect the strongest vertical grayscale boundary in camera 2 and convert its pixel distance from the stored body center using the saved `mm_per_pixel` scale.
- Confirmed that the displayed diameter is the corrected and EMA-filtered state value, not the current frame's raw diameter.
- Confirmed that invalid frames retain the previous valid diameter and only update the status message.
- Noted one formula requiring production confirmation: Neck and Body multiply center-to-edge distance by two, while Endcone currently converts center-to-boundary distance without multiplying by two.
