## Ellipse-Normal Brightness Vertex

- `_fit_ellipse_normal_brightness_vertex()` now performs the final Neck lower-vertex calculation:
  1. Select the configured bottom fraction of the outer crescent contour.
  2. Evaluate the lower branch of the fitted axis-aligned ellipse.
  3. Calculate the normalized outward ellipse normal at each sampled X position.
  4. Use `cv2.remap()` with bilinear interpolation to collect grayscale profiles from the ellipse interior toward the exterior.
  5. Calculate a separate `local maximum - offset` threshold for each profile.
  6. Require at least three consecutive above-threshold samples.
  7. Select the outermost transition to below-threshold brightness.
  8. Interpolate the threshold crossing to a subpixel normal offset.
  9. Convert all crossings back to image coordinates.
  10. Robustly fit a weighted quadratic curve and calculate its vertex.
- Local maximum brightness is used as the fit weight.
- The fitted quadratic must open toward decreasing Y away from its bottom vertex, and the vertex must remain inside both the fitted X range and image bounds.
- If normal search or robust fitting fails, `_find_ellipse()` falls back to the geometric ellipse lower point.
